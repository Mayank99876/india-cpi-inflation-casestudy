## 📌 Project Overview

The **India CPI Inflation Case Study** explores India's Consumer Price Index (CPI) and inflation-related data using Microsoft Excel. The workbook analyzes category-level patterns, food inflation, year-wise changes, the COVID-19 period, and the relationship between crude oil prices and selected CPI categories.

The analysis uses Excel features such as **Power Query, PivotTables, PivotCharts, and formulas** to organize data, compare trends, and summarize findings for business and economic interpretation.

## 🎯 Objectives

- Examine CPI and inflation patterns across years and categories.
- Compare the contribution of major expenditure categories to the overall index.
- Explore food-category trends over a 12-month period.
- Review inflation patterns around the COVID-19 pandemic.
- Investigate how crude oil prices and transport-related indices move over time.
- Present findings in a structured, evidence-based format.

## 📊 Analysis Areas

### 1. Latest-Month Category Comparison
Compared category-level index values and contribution percentages for the latest month available in the workbook.

### 2. Year-wise Inflation Analysis
Reviewed annual patterns and percentage changes in the inflation index, including the higher change observed in 2020 in the workbook's analysis.

### 3. Food Index Trends
Compared monthly food-index averages across a 12-month period and identified changes over time.

### 4. Food Subcategory Analysis
Examined food subcategories such as cereals, meat and fish, eggs, milk, oils and fats, fruits, and vegetables to compare their movement.

### 5. COVID-19 Period Review
Explored inflation and category-level index trends around the COVID-19 period. These are observed patterns and should not be interpreted as proof that the pandemic alone caused the changes.

### 6. Crude Oil and CPI Category Comparison
Compared crude oil price data with selected CPI categories, including transport and communication, to investigate potential relationships. A relationship or correlation does not by itself establish causation.

## 💡 Key Findings Recorded in the Workbook

- Food was identified as a major contributor in the latest-month category comparison.
- The workbook's year-wise analysis marks 2020 as having the highest year-over-year percentage change in the analyzed inflation index.
- The 12-month food-index analysis identifies June 2022 as the highest and December 2022 as the lowest month in that comparison.
- The food subcategory analysis highlights variation among items such as eggs, fruits, and vegetables.
- The workbook compares crude oil prices with transport and communication trends to explore a possible relationship.

*Findings above reflect the calculations and notes in this workbook. They depend on the selected period, data definitions, and aggregation method.*
